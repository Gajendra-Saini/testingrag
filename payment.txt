Payment Service

The payment service processes customer payments after authentication and authorization succeed.

The payment endpoint receives payment details from the API layer.

The payment service validates the payment amount and payment method.

A payment record is created in the database before the external payment provider is called.

The payment service sends the payment request to the external payment provider.

If the provider confirms the payment, the payment status is updated to SUCCESS.

If the provider returns a temporary network error, the payment service retries the request.

The system allows a maximum of three retry attempts.

The delay between retry attempts increases after every failure.

If all retry attempts fail, the payment status is changed to FAILED.

Permanent payment failures are returned to the API caller.
